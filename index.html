About Us

Judex Palm Aquaculture Limited, also known as Judex Palm Fisheries Tech Co, is a pioneering FoodTech enterprise based in Boki, Cross River State. Founded and led by experienced Food Processing Engineers, we specialize in sustainable aquaculture practices that combine traditional Nigerian fish farming methods with modern food technology innovations.

Our facility in Katchuan Irruan serves as a model for responsible fish farming, employing advanced processing techniques to ensure the highest quality standards from pond to plate. We are committed to supporting local food security while creating economic opportunities in rural Nigeria.

At Judex Palm, we don't just farm fish—we're cultivating a sustainable future for Nigerian aquaculture, one pond at a time.
