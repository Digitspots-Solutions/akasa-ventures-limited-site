About Us

Akasa Ventures Limited is a leading manufacturing company based in Boki Local Government Area, Cross River State. We are committed to delivering excellence in manufacturing through cutting-edge processes, quality control, and a dedication to meeting the diverse needs of our clients across Nigeria.

Our strategic location in Cross River State positions us to serve both regional and national markets efficiently. With a focus on innovation, reliability, and customer satisfaction, we have established ourselves as a trusted name in the Nigerian manufacturing sector.
